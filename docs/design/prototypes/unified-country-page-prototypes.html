Unified country page — four iterations

Today the country page is a fixed-height carousel with three siloed tabs (Flavor · Eat & Drink · Culture & Regions). These four prototypes each collapse it into one view. All data is transcribed from the real CN.ts — 10 dishes, 5 drinks, 5 regions — so every count below is honest, including the ugly ones. Use the device toggle to check the phone case; the thesis says the primary moment happens on a phone.

First: does dish → region actually work?

Every region feature depends on this join. China is our best-data country — every dish carries regionalOrigin — so these are ceiling numbers, not floor numbers. Today's rule (detectRegion) strips the parenthetical from the dish's origin and compares to region names. Token-aware also parses the parenthetical in region names into aliases ("Northern China (Beijing & Shandong)" → northern china, beijing, shandong).

Denominator is all 15 items. Two drinks (Doujiang, Baijiu) carry no regionalOrigin at all and can never match — so of the 13 that could, token-aware gets 11. Under today's rule Xinjiang gets zero dishes while being a full-size bubble on the map; token-aware gives it one. The four remaining orphans are Hangzhou and Shaoxing (both Zhejiang — a two-entry alias table away) plus those two origin-less drinks. Conclusion: the region lens is viable now, without waiting on the content batch.

Can region headers carry flavor chips?

There is no region-level flavor data. It is derivable by matching RegionalCuisine.keyIngredients against the ingredients that carry flavorAxes. Coverage is uneven, which is the point of showing it:

Sichuan gets a rich fingerprint; Xinjiang gets one axis. So region headers can carry flavor chips only if the thin case degrades gracefully — shown in iteration 4.

Dish → flavor axis is much weaker

Bridging dish.keyTraits → ingredient names → axes resolves only 5 of 10 dishes, even with loose substring matching. Peking Duck, Xiaolongbao, Jiaozi, Red-Braised Pork and Scallion Pancakes get nothing — their traits ("crispy skin", "soup-filled", "caramelized sugar") name techniques and textures, not ingredients. That is why the Flavor lens is the weakest of the five groupings. Iteration 4 renders it looking bad rather than hiding it; if this lens ships, it needs per-dish axis data from the content batch, not a bridge.
