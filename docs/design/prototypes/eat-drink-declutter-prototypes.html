Eat & Drink, decluttered — three treatments

Same Mexico content in all three. What changes: how much color the filter row and card chips are allowed to use. Icons and labels carry the meaning everywhere; color stops being a per-category costume.

A

One tint for everything

Every chip on every card is the same quiet sage tint — no blue GF, no violet VGT, no red spice box. Active filters are solid sage; inactive are white outlines. Meaning lives entirely in the icon/label. Calm, coherent, still clearly "chips."

B

No boxes at all

The most minimal: card chips dissolve into one muted text line — 📍 · 🌶🌶 · VGT · GF — separated by dots, no backgrounds anywhere. Active filters just turn ink-dark. The cards get very quiet and editorial; the risk is losing the tap-target feel and some glanceability.

C

Hairline outline chips

Chips keep their shape but lose all fills: hairline borders, ink-gray text. Active filters go solid ink (like the Food/Drinks toggle). The middle path — structured like A, restrained like B, and the strongest "modern menu" feel of the three.
