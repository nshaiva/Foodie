Food culture

How meals run

A hearty breakfast, then the comida mid-afternoon as the main meal of the day, often soup, a main and dessert. Dinner is light: antojitos or leftovers.

At the table

Tortillas are utensil and staple. Plates of tacos and antojitos are shared, and lime, salsa and cilantro are always on the table.

Where it comes from

Mesoamerican corn, beans, squash and chilies, cultivated for millennia, met Spanish pork, beef, dairy and wheat.
