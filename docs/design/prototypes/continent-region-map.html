What the numbers changed

Africa split. As one blob it scored 6.48 — worse than random. Morocco and Egypt belong with the Levant, Anatolia and the Caucasus, and that group is the tightest here at 3.66.

Europe did not split. One region scores 3.95; Mediterranean and Northwest halves both come out worse (4.06, 4.12).

The Americas keep a hole. US + Brazil + Argentina is the tightest cluster in the whole dataset (2.31 — grill, smoke, low heat), but Mexico and the Caribbean sit between them and are chile-forward, so they're their own region. The shape is discontiguous on purpose.

Peru fits nowhere. Acidity 9 is an outlier; it rides with the Americas because a region of one isn't worth tapping.
