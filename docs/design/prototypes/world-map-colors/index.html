World map colours

Options for the default world map now that Flavor Match leaves the MVP. Every option shares the same base: a quiet map, and a cuisine takes on its own colour when you hover it or open it. They differ in how the countries you've eaten from show up. Hover any country the app covers, or tap it on a phone.

The current Explored map

For reference. Sage for every cuisine you haven't touched, clay deepening with how much you've tried, and a dark outline on logged countries.

1

Colour only on hover

The literal version: one sand tone for every cuisine, nothing for what you've eaten. The dish plates carry all the colour.

Works

  • The calmest map, and the plates stand out most.
  • Nothing to learn: colour means "you're pointing at this".

Loses

  • No sense of where you've been eating, which is a thesis goal. You'd have to read the list.
2 · Recommended

Your map fills in with colour

Countries you've eaten from take a soft tint of their own colour, stronger the more of their dishes you've tried. Untouched cuisines stay sand. Over time the map becomes a picture of your eating.

Works

  • Colour means one thing: you've been there, and hovering shows it at full strength.
  • Progress without a legend. A pale tint is "a little", a strong one is "a lot".
  • Stays calm early on, when most people have eaten from only a few cuisines.

Watch

  • Needs the four outlier colours evened out (see below), or India and Azerbaijan shout.
  • Neighbours in similar colours (France and South Korea share a blue) are harder to tell apart once both are tinted.
3

A deeper sand for places you've eaten

One colour family: explored cuisines are a deeper sand, stepping darker with progress. Own colours appear only on hover.

Works

  • The most restrained way to keep the explored signal.
  • No clashing colours, whatever the palette.

Loses

  • Close to today's clay ramp, just quieter. It doesn't feel much like "my map".
  • Light steps of one tone are hard to read at world size.
4

Progress plates on the map

The map stays plain sand. Countries you've eaten from get a small progress plate, the same ring the panel's list uses, filled in the country's colour.

Works

  • Reuses a mark the app already has, so map and list agree.
  • Exact progress at a glance, not just "some".

Loses

  • Competes with the dish plates for the same spot over each country.
  • Small countries (Lebanon, Jamaica) get a ring bigger than the country.
