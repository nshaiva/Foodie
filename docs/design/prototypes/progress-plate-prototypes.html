Progress plates — "% of dishes tried" as the logo mark

Three fill treatments for the dot-plate. Decided constraints: styled like the logo's concentric rings, fills with progress, hidden at 0%. Drag the slider at the bottom of each card to sweep the fill. Terracotta shown; on country surfaces it could take the country's palette color instead.

A

Pie plate

The literal read of "30% tried = 30% of the plate filled": the plate's inner well fills as a pie wedge, clockwise from 12 o'clock, inside the pale logo rings. Continuous — every new dish visibly nudges it. This is the closest to what you described.

B

Course by course

The rings light up in thirds, from the center out: center dot solid after the first third, mid ring after two thirds, full rim at (nearly) all tried. Quantized — less precise, but it makes each ring feel like a course you've cleared, and it's the most "logo" of the three at small sizes.
