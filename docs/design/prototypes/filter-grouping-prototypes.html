Filter grouping — three structures

Same filters in all three (food mode shown): view (All/Tried/Want), diet (VGT/VG/GF), spice scale, popularity (Local/Tourist), Dessert. What changes is how they're chunked. Sage = active, matching the one-tint chip system.

A

Two rows, labeled groups

Row one is the view — the "which dishes am I looking at" tier, bigger pills. Row two is refinement, chunked with tiny uppercase labels. Everything stays visible; the labels do the organizing that dividers were failing to do. Costs one extra line of height.
